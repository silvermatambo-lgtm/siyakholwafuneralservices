WHO WE ARE

Compassion, dignity, trust

Siyakholwa Funeral Services is a 100% Black-owned funeral service provider based in the Eastern Cape. Our goal is to offer families dependable funeral arrangements, affordable cover enquiries and compassionate support.

Death can be unexpected. Our approach brings together funeral planning, caring body collection and dignified service, and guidance on member benefits and available funding options.

Our promise: respectful support, clear communication and a farewell that honours your loved one.
